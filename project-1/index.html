My walk around the River

I took a walk near the river. I started to notice how nature and human life exist in the same place.

LOOK UP

I saw birds and a helicopter in the sky.

A small bird was sitting on a sign.

LOOK ACROSS

Some people were kayaking on the river.

I saw a large white yacht near the dock.

LOOK DOWN

I noticed old road lines and cracks.

I saw small plants growing between stones.

WHAT I FELT

I think this area is busy but also relaxing. I like the contrast between nature and human life.
